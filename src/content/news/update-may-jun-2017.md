<p>The month of May was dominated by Big Church Day Out&nbsp;2017, a two-legged music festival for which Mark directed 32 hours worth of broadcast programming. The shows will be hosted by Lavinia and Adaeze of <em>Juicy Ladies</em>&nbsp; fame, and feature the main stage acts - including the likes of Michelle Williams and Mary Mary - interviews with the top performers, stories from the punters, and all the sights and sounds of the festival.</p>
<p>In addition, Mark managed to fit in a music video shoot for newcomers The Vile Assembly (thanks in no small part to the sterling work of producer Jonny Evers). Their debut single, <em>Suicide Feast</em>, will be released on Vial Records on 28th July.</p>
<p>On top of all that, Mark directed two brand new series for TBN UK: the 14-part series <em>Now Living</em>, hosted by Javen, and 8-parter <em>Embracing The Challenge</em>, hosted by Gabriel Chan.</p>
