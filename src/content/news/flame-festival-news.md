<p>Described by SaMoIndie Film Festival as a "...piece of eye candy that moves with a cat-like grace and the confidence of a seductress",<em>&nbsp;FLAME</em>, the short film which Mark made in collaboration with Sapphires Model Management, has received official selections&nbsp;at a number of festivals around the world. In addition, it has been nominated Best Dramatic Film at the Short Film Awards 2014, and Best Narrational Short at the Toronto Film &amp; Video Awards 2014.</p>
