<p>The Nest Egg is a short film which Mark assistant directed back in 2014. Produced by Martina Silcock, directed by Lucy Patrick Ward, and penned by Lee Sutton, it has been touring the festivals over the last nine months, and has recently picked up a couple more&nbsp;official selections, including at the DC Shorts Film Festival in Washington, DC. The film has previously won the Jury prize for Best Short Film at the Noida International Film Festival.</p>
