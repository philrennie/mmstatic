<p>Mark's summer has been somewhat hectic. A new series for TBN, "Staying Power", kicked things off, before Mark ducked off to the Swedish countryside to hang out with friends and lend a hand at their local street art festival they put on over July and August.</p>
<p>This was rapidly followed by a two week trip to the Middle East, where Mark and colleagues visited Israel and Jordan to film a documentary taking a look at religious persecution in the region. And, amongst some further TV work, the month was rounded off with the follow-up music video for The Vile Assembly: "Gone".</p>
