<p>Mark spent today shooting&nbsp;the Are You Listening? festival in Reading. The event is a multi-venue music festival which takes place on a variety of stages across the town - attendees can pick and choose their way through the line-up. And it all helps out charity Reading Mencap, set up to support people with learning difficulties in the local area.</p>
<p>This year marked the 5th anniversary, and punters were treated to performances from the&nbsp;likes of Anna Meredith, The Amazons and Flamingods.</p>
