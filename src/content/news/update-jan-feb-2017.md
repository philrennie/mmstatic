<p>Mark has kicked off the year with a raft of new content for TBN UK. A brand-new series, <em>Juicy Ladies</em>, focusses on urban Christian culture and will hit TV screens and streaming services in May 2017. The show is presented by Lavinia Goddard and Adaeze Chiwoko of Vinejuice Radio, who bring a whole heap of Juice to proceedings. Expect live music, riotous chat and ridiculous games.</p>
<p>In February Mark travelled to the North East to shoot a series of short personal&nbsp;stories with TBN UK viewers. The mini documentary-style pieces will air on the channel and on social media&nbsp;in the coming weeks.</p>
