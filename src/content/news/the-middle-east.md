<p>Mark has just awoken from 15hrs straight sleep off the back of a 2-week long trip to the Middle East to shoot a feature-length documentary for TBN UK. The film will explore&nbsp;religious persecution across the major monotheistic religions, and has seen Mark meet residents of Bethlehem living alongside the wall, major players in the reconciliation efforts of Israel and Palestine, and Iraqi refugees trying to rebuild their lives in Amman, Jordan. The film is slated for release some time in the new year.</p>
