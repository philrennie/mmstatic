<p>Hello Wembley! Mark spent the day shooting with&nbsp;Brian "Head" Welch for TBN UK. Welch is the guitarist for KoRn, one of the foremost Nu-metal bands in the world. Starting out on the streets around Wembley Arena meeting fans, the day moved to the TBN UK London Studios for an interview with the renowned performer, before moving to the 20,000 capacity Wembley Arena for a double-headline show with Limp Bizkit. After the gig, Welch met with a handful of lucky fans to share with them his story and journey to faith.</p>
