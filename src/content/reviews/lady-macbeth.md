<p>The virtues of simple storytelling exude in this period tale set in the rugged northeast and based upon Nikolai Leskov's Lady Macbeth of Mtsensk District.</p>
<p>Thrust into a phoney marriage as part of a meagre land deal, Katherine is housebound and alone. When her uncaring husband and wretched father-in-law both depart on business, she takes the opportunity to modernise etiquette and fraternise with the household staff, with severe consequences.</p>
<p>A beautifully understated film on all fronts, and highly recommended.</p>
