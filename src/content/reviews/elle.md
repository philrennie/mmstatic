<p>With a back catalogue featuring some of the greatest satires of our time, it should be expected that Paul Verhoeven's <em>Elle</em> is worthwhile viewing. But this is a far more complex and mature offering than his most famous Hollywood works such as <em>Robocop</em> and <em>Starship Troopers</em>.</p>
<p>With Isabelle Huppert -- one of the great actors of our time -- in the lead role, the viewer is treated to a fascinatingly&nbsp;nuanced performance: Michele's story is brutal, both historically and presently, but she is fantastically strong despite her many ordeals. The depth of the central character is ably&nbsp;reinforced by the work of the supporting cast, who together conjure something of a whodunnit which will have you hooked from beginning to end.</p>
