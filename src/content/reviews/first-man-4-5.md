<p>Damien Chazelle's 4th feature is a biopic charting Neil Armstrong's passage from NACA test pilot to lunar pioneer.&nbsp;The film splits its time between [spoilers] the often tragic advance of the Space Race, and Armstrong's sometimes stoic&nbsp;response to the personal and political events taking place around him. For that reason, Ryan Gosling is well-cast in the lead role, yet also has&nbsp;one or two&nbsp;opportunities for more expressive reactions,&nbsp;whilst Claire Foy cements herself as one of the leading actors of the moment.</p>
<p>Once again, Chazelle serves up a very definite vision, seamlessly melding sound with picture. Where, in <em>Whiplash</em>, the cut mirrored the impressionistic jazz movements and, in <em>La La Land</em>, the camera sashayed along with the performances, here the&nbsp;framing is tight and every rivet (mechanical or emotional) is explored in rude close up.&nbsp;At the same time, the sound design underscores the fragility of what is at stake, on both a technical and, more importantly, human level.</p>
